Connect Trakt

What you watch in TopShelf is added to your Trakt history, and what you've watched elsewhere (in Nuvio, Stremio, on your phone…) comes back: part-watched titles join Continue watching, and watched episodes get their ticks.

1 Make a Trakt app for your TV (once, 2 minutes)

  1. Open developer.trakt.tv › My Apps › New app and sign in to Trakt.
  2. Name: TopShelf · choose Production if it asks.
  3. Redirect URI: urn:ietf:wg:oauth:2.0:oob (ignore the "insecure redirect" warning — this one is right for a TV)
  4. Leave the rest as it is and create the app.
  5. Copy the Client ID into the box below. If Trakt shows a Client Secret, copy that too; newer apps say Not issued — then leave it empty.
