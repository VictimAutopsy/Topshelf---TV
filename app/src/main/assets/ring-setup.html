Connect your Ring doorbell

When someone rings, the TV shows their picture and pauses what's playing.

1 Sign in to Ring

Your password goes from this page to the TV on your home Wi-Fi, and from the TV to Ring, once. TopShelf doesn't keep it: it keeps only the sign-in Ring hands back. Ring doesn't offer other apps an official way in, so this signs in the way Ring's own Android app does.

2 The code from Ring

Ring sends a code by text or email.

3 Done

The TV is listening for your doorbell. It shows as "TopShelf" in the Ring app under Control Center › Authorized client devices, where you can remove it.
