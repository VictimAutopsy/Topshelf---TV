Connect Google Drive

Your TV will upload recordings to your Google Drive as it records them, then play them from there — freeing up the TV's storage.

1 Make a Google app for your TV (once, about 5 minutes)

Google only lets registered apps use Drive, so you make a free one of your own. TopShelf can then see only the files it creates (a “TopShelf Recordings” folder) — nothing else in your Drive. Sign in with the Google account whose Drive you want to use.

  1. First, 2-Step Verification: Google Cloud now requires it. If you see “Google Cloud access blocked”, turn it on at myaccount.google.com/signinoptions/twosv, wait a few minutes, then come back here.
  2. Create a project: open console.cloud.google.com/projectcreate, name it TopShelf, tap Create. Wait for it, then make sure it's the project selected at the top of the page.
  3. Turn on Drive: open the Google Drive API page and tap Enable.
  4. Set up sign-in: open Google Auth Platform → Get started. App name TopShelf, your email as the support email → Next. Audience: External → Next. Your email again → Next → agree → Create.
  5. Publish it (so the TV stays signed in): open Audience → Publish app → Confirm. It should say In production. If Google first asks for a home page and privacy policy (Branding), use a public page of your own (for example a GitHub repository's README), add its domain under Authorized domains, and leave the logo empty — a logo makes Google want to review the app.
  6. Make the TV's key: open Clients → Create client. Application type: TVs and Limited Input devices. Name: TopShelf TV → Create.
  7. Copy the Client ID and Client secret it shows into the boxes below. Missed the secret? Tap the client's name on the Clients page, then the ⓘ icon at the top right (on a phone) → Client secrets → + Add secret, and copy the new one straight away.

2 Sign in and allow access

Open Google's TV sign-in page, sign in, and enter this code:

— — — —
Open google.com/device

Google will say “Google hasn't verified this app” — that's expected, since it's your own app. Tap Continue, then allow TopShelf to see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app. This page notices when you're done.

✓ Done

You can close this page. New recordings upload to TopShelf Recordings in your Drive while they record; once a recording is finished and all of it is on Drive, it's removed from the TV and plays from Drive. Change that in Settings › Live TV › Google Drive on the TV.
