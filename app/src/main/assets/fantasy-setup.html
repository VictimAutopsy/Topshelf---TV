Connect Yahoo Fantasy

Your TV will show alerts when your players score, make big plays or get hurt, and each game's final.

1 Create a Yahoo app (once, about 5 minutes)

Yahoo only lets registered apps read fantasy teams, so you make a free one for your TV.

  1. Open developer.yahoo.com/apps/create and sign in with the Yahoo account your league uses.
  2. Application name: anything, for example TopShelf TV.
  3. If it asks for an application type, pick Installed Application (or Confidential Client).
  4. Redirect URI(s): https://localhost:8080
  5. API permissions: tick Fantasy Sports and choose Read. (This one matters: without it Yahoo signs you in but refuses to share your team — “Yahoo 403”. You can add it later: open your app at developer.yahoo.com/apps, tick it, save, then sign in again.)
  6. Tap Create App, then copy the Client ID and Client Secret it shows into the boxes below.

2 Sign in and allow access

Sign in to Yahoo

After you tap Agree, Yahoo sends you to a localhost page that won't load — that's expected. Copy the whole address from the address bar (it contains code=) and paste it here. If Yahoo shows you a code instead, paste the code.

✓ Done

You can close this page. Alerts appear on the TV while TopShelf is on screen (Home and Live TV), never over other apps. Change which alerts you get in Settings › Sports on the TV.
