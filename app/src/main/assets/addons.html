Addons

The same kind of addons Nuvio and Stremio use. On the TV, choose a movie or show in Movies or TV Shows to see the streams your addons find for it (for example from your debrid service), and play them in TopShelf's player.

Add an addon

Using Nuvio? Open Nuvio on the TV › Settings › Add-ons › Manage from phone to see your addons there, copy each one's link and paste it here. Debrid addons put your debrid key in their link — keep these links private.

Copy your addons from Stremio

Signs in to your Stremio account once, only to read your addon list. Your password isn't kept — not on the TV, not anywhere.
